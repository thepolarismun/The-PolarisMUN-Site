The Polaris MUN · Official Brochure Committees & Agendas
Section I
Committees &
Agendas

The Polaris MUN convenes five committees spanning fictional, security, legislative, and human-rights dimensions of global governance. Each committee has been assigned a distinct and substantive agenda designed to challenge delegates to think critically, negotiate strategically, and draft meaningful resolutions.

01
MSC Multiverse Stability Council Complete Crisis
Agenda
Deliberation upon the destabilization of the multiverse and measures to prevent catastrophic inter-reality collapse.
02
DISEC Disarmament & International Security Committee Partial Crisis
Agenda
Deliberation on Gray Zone Warfare, AI-Driven Disinformation, and Strategic Militarization in Emerging Arctic Corridors.
03
UNGA United Nations General Assembly
Agenda
Deliberation on strategic tensions in West Asia with focus on recurring cross-border hostilities and escalating military posturing among regional actors.
04
UNSC United Nations Security Council
Agenda
Combating Maritime Piracy in the Gulf of Guinea and Its Impact on Global Trade Security.
05
UNHRC United Nations Human Rights Council
Agenda
Deliberation on systemic human rights concerns arising from immigration enforcement, border control, migrant detention, and deportation practices, and their impact on the rights and dignity of migrants, refugees, and asylum seekers worldwide.

Section II
A Note from
the Founders

We'll be honest — we started this as delegates. Between the three of us, we've sat in enough committees to know every version of how it goes. The sessions where you lose the room completely. The ones where your entire position falls apart thirty minutes in and you have to rebuild it from scratch. The moments where you're under so much pressure you can't think straight, and somehow — somehow — you find the argument anyway. And occasionally, the sessions where everything clicks and you walk out wondering how you pulled that off.

That cycle — losing points, losing arguments, getting backed into a corner, and figuring your way out — that's actually the whole thing. That's what makes you better. Not the wins. The fact that every time you sit down in a committee, you're a slightly sharper version of the person who sat down last time. You might not feel it in the moment, but it's happening

We built Polaris because we wanted a conference that actually creates those moments. Not a comfortable, predictable two days — but the kind where you're genuinely tested. Where you have to hold two completely opposing ideas in your head at once and still find a way forward. Where the pressure is real, the delegates across the table are prepared, and nothing is handed to you.

This is a conference by delegates, for delegates. We know what it feels like to be in your seat. And we built this for the version of you that's going to walk out better than the one that walked in.

Our Promise

It won't always be comfortable. You'll lose points you thought were airtight. You'll get outmanoeuvred. There will be a moment where the room turns and you're the one scrambling. But if you stay in it — every single time — you'll walk out better than you came in. That's the only promise we're making. And we're confident enough in it to put our names on this.
