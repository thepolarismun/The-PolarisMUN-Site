A Note to Delegates

This background guide is intended to familiarise delegates with the core issues before the Multiverse Stability Council. You are expected to arrive prepared with a thorough understanding of your assigned reality's position, its relationship to neighbouring dimensions, and its stake in the outcome of these deliberations.

The crisis before the Council is not merely diplomatic — it is existential. Delegates are reminded that the decisions made in this chamber may determine the survival or destruction of entire realities.

Delegate Preparation

Research your assigned reality, understand its unique vulnerabilities to the Narrative Collapse, and come prepared to negotiate, persuade, and compromise in the interest of multiversal survival.

MSC — Multiverse Stability Council Section I · Introduction
Section I
Introduction to
the Crisis

For countless ages, realities existed independently of one another, separated by stable dimensional barriers that ensured the balance of existence across the multiverse. Each universe followed its own laws of physics, magic, time, and causality, remaining isolated from parallel realities.

While theories regarding alternate timelines and parallel universes existed within several realities, large-scale interaction between universes remained rare and highly restricted. This equilibrium was the foundation upon which all of existence rested.

This balance has now begun to collapse.

Section II
The Narrative Collapse

Several years prior to the formation of the Multiverse Stability Council, isolated anomalies began emerging throughout multiple realities. Initially appearing insignificant, these events were the earliest signs of a catastrophic systemic failure — now known as The Narrative Collapse.

Early Warning Signs
  • Individuals recalling memories from lives they had never lived
  • Objects appearing from unknown realities
  • Temporal inconsistencies and paradox events
  • Spontaneous manifestation of "variants" — alternate selves
Expert Consensus

Scientists, sorcerers, artificial intelligences, and cosmic entities across numerous universes reached a singular conclusion: the barriers separating realities were weakening. As anomalies increased in frequency, it became clear that no single universe was immune.

Section III
Incursions
Incursion frequency: Accelerating · Multiple universes at critical risk

The weakening of dimensional barriers has led to catastrophic inter-universal collisions known as Incursions. An Incursion occurs when two universes begin occupying the same dimensional space simultaneously. Once initiated, an Incursion destabilises both realities — and cannot be halted without decisive intervention.

Possible Outcomes of an Incursion
I
One Survives
One universe is destroyed; the other survives in a destabilised state.
II
Merger
Both universes merge into a single, profoundly unstable reality.
III
Total Annihilation
Both realities involved are completely and permanently destroyed.

Initially rare, Incursions have become increasingly frequent and unpredictable. Entire universes have already ceased to exist. The rate of collapse continues to accelerate, and without coordinated intervention, the process may become irreversible.

Critical Assessment

The frequency of Incursions has now surpassed the Council's capacity for individual response. Delegates must devise systemic, multilateral solutions — not merely reactive measures. Time is the scarcest resource before this body.

Section IV
Present Situation

The multiverse currently exists in a state of severe instability. Reality fractures have emerged across countless dimensions, allowing unrestricted inter-universal movement, unauthorised timeline interference, and the appearance of dangerous variants from alternate realities.

Confirmed Consequences
  • Unstable portals between universes
  • Timeline corruption
  • Paradox events
  • Reality overlap
Escalating Threats
  • Uncontrolled distribution of advanced technologies and magical artifacts
  • Escalating conflicts between universes competing for survival
  • Appearance of hostile variants
  • Imminent destruction of numerous realities
Geopolitical Dimension

The crisis has introduced a dangerous zero-sum dynamic between realities. As universes collapse, surviving realities compete for dimensional territory, energy sources, and habitable dimensions — threatening to transform a natural disaster into an inter-universal conflict.

Several rogue entities and organisations have begun exploiting the instability for purposes of conquest, resource acquisition, or ideological domination. Their interference further accelerates the collapse and complicates any coordinated response.

Numerous realities now face imminent destruction. The window for effective intervention is narrowing with every passing moment.

MSC — Multiverse Stability Council Section V · The Council
Section V
The Multiverse
Stability Council

Recognising the scale of the crisis, representatives from across the multiverse assembled to establish the Multiverse Stability Council (MSC). The purpose of the Council is to coordinate multiversal efforts aimed at preventing total collapse and restoring stability across realities.

Mandate and Responsibilities
  • Regulating inter-universal travel and dimensional transit
  • Preventing further Incursions through coordinated intervention
  • Stabilising collapsing timelines across affected realities
  • Investigating the origin and root cause of the Narrative Collapse
  • Establishing protocols for interaction between universes
  • Determining emergency measures necessary for multiversal survival
Internal Tensions

Cooperation within the Council remains fragile. Many delegates prioritise the survival of their own universe above all else. Others seek to exploit the instability to expand their influence. Several delegates openly question whether collapse can still be prevented at all.

The Council's effectiveness depends entirely on the willingness of its members to subordinate narrow national — or rather, universal — interests to the collective good. History across every reality suggests this is among the most difficult of diplomatic challenges.

MSC — Multiverse Stability Council Section VII · Questions for Consideration
Section VII
Questions for
Consideration

Delegates are expected to engage meaningfully with the following questions during debate. These are intended as a framework, not a limitation — the Council's deliberations may evolve beyond them.

  • What mechanisms should the Council establish to detect, monitor, and respond to Incursions before they reach critical mass?
  • How should the MSC regulate inter-universal travel without violating the sovereignty of individual realities?
  • What protocols should govern the identification, containment, or treatment of variants, given the paradox risks they pose?
  • Should the Council prioritise investigating the root cause of the Narrative Collapse, or focus resources on immediate crisis response?
  • How should the Council address rogue entities and organisations actively exploiting the collapse? What enforcement powers does — or should — the MSC possess?
  • In a scenario where not all realities can be saved, how should the Council determine which universes to prioritise, and on what grounds?
  • What is the long-term governance structure of the MSC, and how should its authority be balanced against the autonomy of member realities?
Conclusion

The multiverse now stands at the edge of irreversible collapse. Whether through diplomacy, sacrifice, regulation, conquest, or conflict, the decisions made within this Council may determine the survival — or destruction — of existence itself.

Final Reminder

Every delegate represents not just their reality, but every living being within it. The weight of that responsibility must guide every resolution, every clause, and every vote cast before this Council.
