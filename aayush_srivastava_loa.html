Appointment Terms & Conditions
Binding upon acceptance of Letter of Appointment

By accepting this Letter of Appointment, the board member agrees to be bound by the following terms and conditions for the duration of their service at The Polaris MUN 2026.

1.
Attendance & Punctuality
The board member is required to be present for the full duration of the conference on both days — 27 and 28 June 2026. Reporting time will be communicated separately. Failure to attend without prior written notice to the founding team will result in immediate revocation of this appointment.
2.
Conduct & Professionalism
Board members are expected to maintain a professional standard of conduct at all times — in committee, during breaks, and in any communication with delegates. Behaviour deemed disruptive, disrespectful, or prejudicial to any delegate will result in removal from the board without notice.[Exempt from internal communications between the board and staff]
3.
Impartiality
Board members must exercise strict impartiality in all committee proceedings. Personal relationships with delegates must not influence any decision, award, or action taken within committee. Any conflict of interest must be disclosed to the founding team prior to the conference.
4.
Preparation
Board members are expected to be thoroughly prepared on the subject matter of their assigned committee. As the background guide is authored by the board itself, it is expected that each member has a comprehensive understanding of the agenda and all material relevant to their role before the conference begins.
5.
Confidentiality
Any internal communications, crisis updates, award deliberations, or organisational decisions shared with the board member in their capacity as a board member are strictly confidential and must not be disclosed to delegates or external parties.
6.
Revocation
The founding team reserves the right to revoke this appointment at any time — before or during the conference — if the board member is found to be in breach of any of these terms, or if their conduct is deemed contrary to the interests of the conference or its delegates.
7.
Honorarium & Compensation
You, the vice-chairperson of DISEC, will receive an honorarium of ₹800 (Rupees Eight Hundred) upon successful completion of their duties across both days of the conference. This amount is forfeited entirely in the event of voluntary resignation, early departure, or removal from the board for breach of these terms. The honorarium will be disbursed by the founding team after the conclusion of the conference.
8.
Acceptance
Acceptance of this Letter of Appointment constitutes full agreement to these terms and conditions.
